18th May, 2000

Hello all,

Era Online 2 is called off. Reason for this is because Fantasia Studios, the makers of Era Online 1, has now been officially established as a commercial company in Norway, with offices etc. Our web site www.fantasiastudios.com will also be a portal, where you can read up to date news, stories etc about programming, gaming, graphics and other news. We will also invite to contests, discussions etc. However, to your suprise, Fantasia Studios first commercial product will be a online RPG like Ultima Online with top down/isometric 3d rendered graphics and have the typical fantasy elements etc. We have a team of 4 in-office staff as well as 5 out of office staff. We will not release ANY information on this product, and it has nothing to do with Era Online. It will be a commercial game with Ultima Online standard, and will be pay to play. Our goal is to make it atleast twice as good as Ultima Online etc. Sorry for any dissapointed souls that were hoping for a Era Online 2, free to play.

Era Online final version will hit then net with a boom sometime in the next few weeks. We are making the final touches to the game right now, and our main concern is server space. The arrival of EO final version will be announced all over the net, and I will probably give the responsobility of EO to another trusted, dedicated person when its out, so I can go on to other projects including Fantasia Studios and the new game.

Friendly,
Erling Ellingsen

9th May, 2000

FINAL BETA ?

The rumors has been flowing lately and here are the official word.
FINAL BETA will hit as soon as the server are built and are ready.
The FINAL BETA will contain a few new features, but mostly the old
ones, and all the bugs found in BETA 2 are fixed, however dont expect
a completely bug free version. However, there is no FINAL BETA. This
release will be Era Online FINAL VERSION with and without the bugs.
So stay tuned, we will still update, fix and add to it however, but
it is the official final version.

ERA ONLINE 2 ?

Yeah ! As soon as FINAL VERSION of the original Era Online has been
launched we are starting the Era Online 2: Renaissance Of An Era project.
We have already started forming the development team, and most of the
team members are new ones, that had nothing to do with the original.
The web site for the game will be online around the release of the final
version of the original Era Online. We don't have too much information yet.

All registered newsletter subscribers of this newsletter, will also recive
information on Era Online 2 etc. 

Era Online 2: Renaissance Of An Era, will be totally stand alone. We will
rewrite all history, all names etc. It has nothing to do with the original.
We will redo all the originals graphics to 3d rendered images, plus adding
alot more. The world will be over 5 times bigger than the original world.
We will add more races, more classes, more skills, tons of new objects and
spells and tons of new features. The game will be more than a new game, than
a sequel. All we can say now is that Era Online 2: Renaissance Of An Era will
be so awsome, that you will be dying to play it once we release more info
on it. Release date is not in the near future at all. We would hope before
January 2001. No promises. However, we will kep the development very open and
let the fans in on everything to make sure everyone is having fun.


The original Era Online will run as long as people play it, until Era Online 2
launches. And probably even after that as well. Whats wrong in having two Era's
online ? However, my attention will be totally towards Era Online 2. I have
learned from my mistakes on the original project, and Era Online 2 will be quite
a different matter. Much more organized and professional, and the game itself will
be up to commercial standards, however it will be free to play just as the original.

FEEL THE POWER OF THE ERA GAMES ! :)

the graphic are saved in 256-bit BMP files. You are to keep the same size of the objects
as the one sent to you in advance.

Interested ? Visit www.eraonline.net to check out our current graphics.
Contact admin@eraonline.net NOW !

Era Online is a multiplayer massive online RPG, free to play. We have a fan base
containing hundreds of people, the web site have had over 4 000 000 accesses in 6
months, and the test runs have been crowded with people. The game is FULL of features,

However, the graphics are so and so, and thats why we hope to optimize everything to 3D
quality, plus making the game to a TOTALLY commercial standard, however still keeping it
