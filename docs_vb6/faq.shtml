Era Online FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

By Erling Ellingsen

 

Q: When is Era Online going to be released as  full version ?
A: Sometime in August/September 2000.

Q: How much will EO cost?
A: Nothing ! It will be free to both play and download!

Q: Why is Era Online free to play?
A: Era Online is free to play because the server we will be using if offered to us for free, and also because all the members of the development team do this out of interest and the joy of creating and seeing other people enjoy their work. So Era Online will cost absolutely nothing to play or download!

Q: What kind of game is Era Online ?
A: Era Online is a pure RPG that runs online. It has many of the traditional RPG elements but also new ones. Era Online is as the name says, a online game. Thousands of players can log into the same world and play with each other in a huge fantastic world with beautiful graphics. The world is mainly inhabitant by real people all over the world but you can also find NPC`s and also many kinds of monsters and animals. Era Online is a medieval fantasy game where almost everything can come true.

Q: How is the community in Era Online?
A: Era Online's community is great. The game world called Menath, is inhabitant by REAL people. There is a emperor and a high council. The world is driven by supply and demand and also has a great reputation system. Players can chat with each other, fight with each other, explore with each other and marry each other ! Players can visit concert places where player bards can perform and they can participate in many of the quests, competitions and festivals real players and Game Masters put together. Also there is a real newspaper in the game and the whole world is so dynamic! Everything can happen ! Everything WILL happen !

Q: Can you tell me more about the world in Era Online?
A: The world in EO (Era Online) is called Menath. It has a very rich history and background and the world is stunning graphically and atmospherically. There are 10 villages, 10 dungeons and 7 huge cities. In between all this you can find vast deserts, great dark forests, seas, mountains, snowy areas and also jungle ! Also around the world there is monsters and animals of all kinds scattered around. You can find castles, farms, temples, graveyards and everything else which you would find in a medieval fantasy world. The world itself is absolutely huge ! It covers thousands of screens. The world is built up in zones. One zone can host a entire city ! And there is about 200 zones in Menath ! Also, the world is full of royalty and beggars, warriors and tailors and everything else ! Its really a living breathing world !

Q: Does all places in Menath look the same ?
A: Absolutely no ! All towns and villages look different and each have their own special form of architecture. Each city and part of the world has their special house types. So traveling is very interesting since the world is very very unique and never seem to look the same.

Q: What classes and races can you find in EO ?
A: There are totally 20 classes/occupations in EO at this time. This range from baker to assassin and wizard to blacksmith. You can find a entire list over all classes/occupations in the WHAT IS ERA ONLINE section on our site. There is currently 5 races in EO. And of them there is 4 playable ones. The playable ones is Dark Elves, Wood Elves, Humans and Haakis. The currently unplayable one is the Dwarven race. However this may change. Also, we will implement new classes/occupations and races after the release.

Q: Is there quests in Era Online ?
A: Absolutely ! You can find minor unimportant quests like bringing something to one NPC to another and bigger quests alike slaying a dragon or slaying a evil necromancer that has been a pain for years. Its the EO community !

Q: Can you interact with NPCs ?
A: Yes you can. You can ask them if they want to trade, you can give them things, you can ask them about quests and gossip and also much more. You can also fight them.

Q: Can you find all NPCs ?
A: No you cant. Not all NPC`s is killable, but most of them are.

Q: Is there a plot ?
A: No. The players is simply presented with a rich history and background and from there anything can happen. Players can do whatever they want and nothing will stop them. Its a ongoing world which will be online 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Q: What is the minimum requirements ?
A: Those have changed a bit since the beginning. The current requirements are something like these (may change):

Pentium 233 32 RAM DirectX 6.0 50 MB hard drive space Sound Card (Optional) Keyboard Mouse Windows 95 or 98 or higher Internet Connection

Q: 50 MB hard drive space ? Must players download 50 MB ?
A: Absolutely not ! You download a compressed version ala 17 MB.

Q: Will there be multiple servers ?
A: We will start with one server at the beginning but if we need more, we will put up more !

Q: How many players can one server support ?
A: Probably up to 1000 players or more. This will be tested in the Alpha.

Q: What RPG elements is found in EO ?
A: Most of the traditional RPG elements is to be found. There are classic races, classes, weapons, spells, magic system and the general atmosphere of the game.

Q: What kind of monsters can i encounter in EO ?
A: You can encounter monsters like orcs, fanatic necromancers, evil angels, giant snakes, skeleton warriors, zombies, ogres, spiders and a whole lot more. And we will be adding new monsters very often.

Q: How does combat system work ?
A: As in most RPG`s. Very simple. You walk up to your pray (if not your pray hunt down) and you begin slashing away. You will use the weapon equipped in your hand and you will start fighting. There are also combat skills like tactics, parrying and swordmanship that comes into play when fighting.

Q: How many NPC types is there in EO ?
A: There are at this time about 70 different NPC types of each race.

Q: How does the guards in EO work ?
A: Very realistic ! They will patrol the streets and when someone does something illegal, then they will start running at them and hunt them down. There are no instant kill guards in EO. You may run away from them or even manage to kill them. However, guards may team up on you just as monsters do. However, friends can help you and stand in their way so the guards will have to run around them and so on. Very realistic.

Q: How is player-killing handled in EO ?
A: At first we was thinking of implementing a PK switch as in EverQuest but we decided not to because many players was against it. However, we found that players want free PK kill, so thats how it is. Kill anyone you want.

Q: How will the leveling system work ?
A: When you have killed many monsters and gained experience or gained experience in another way, you will eventually level up. However, there are no track of levels in EO. You simply gain experience, and are boosted up. There isnt any official leveling. This is to keep the RP feeling intact. I hate it when people run around, "Oh only 4k to next lvl!"

Q: What about skills ? What skills are there ? How do i get better in them ?
A: There are about 30 different skills at this time and you can find a complete list at our website under the WHAT IS ERA ONLINE section. You get better in them when you use them and also when you train in them. However, you cannot pay a NPC and then they'll train you. No no. You gain Training Points when leveling up. You gain about 5 points by each level. Then you can go to a NPC trainer and use those Training Points in any of the skills. There are at this time no training dolls either so players can hack and slash on a doll forever and expect to be a super player by some hours.

Q: Are there spells in EO ?
A: Of course !

Q: How many spells exactly ?
A: Many !

Q: How does the spell system work ? Mana ?
A: If you play a character which is computable with spells you will begin with a few spells and you may buy or learn new ones by time. Whenever you cast a spell you will uses mana or magic as many calls it, and spells require a certain amount of mana.

Q: Are there any player guilds and in game guilds ?
A: Only player driven for now. Players can start their own clans, and the monarch of the clan can declare war on other clans and alliances with other clans. If your in a clan and you gain experience, some of the experience goes to the clan, and regularily the experience is distributed to the clan members. *Clan System Not In Until FULL VERSION*

Q: How will you support them ?
A: Well, if they want a guild hall they will have to buy a guild hall then they may put it up. We will help them establish and introduce them to the guild making system.

Q: Can you tell us more about monster AI ?
A: The monsters act differently. Some ignores other player while others smell players from long distances and hunts them down. Also monsters may team up on you.

Q: What about loot ? Can everybody loot your monster ?
A: If there is loot on the corpse, its placed around the corpse. So be quick to pick.

Q: Can players create parties ?
A: There isnt a ingame system for this yet.

Q: Can player buy houses ? How will this work ?
A: Players can go to a architect and buy a house deed. When he has found a good place to build the house he puts it there and then he may furnish the house. Players can either make or buy furniture of all kinds.

Q: Can player create town ?
A: In a way yes. If many players has made lots of houses in one area then Fantasia Studios may declare it a new village or town.

Q: What will follow with the downloadable game ? Graphics ?
A: The player will download the client, graphics, music, sound and a part of the game world. The part of the game world that is on the client cannot be changed.

Q: Can i alter the graphics to make my own style game ?
A: Absolutely not. Though its possible to change the graphics, it would screw up the game and non of the other players would be able to see the graphics you changed. Also, we will not tolerate people hacking, cheating or altering EO.

Q: Are there game supervisors ? And what do they do ?
A: Yes, there are many GMs (Game Masters). They will supervise the game at all times and report and strike down on people breaking the code of conduct. Also, GMs will be there to help and guide players.

Q: What is Code Of Conduct ?
A: A set of rules we must make to control what is legal in the game (Out of character). Code Of Conduct says for example that you may not harass any player or talk sexually explicit language.

Q: What will happen to people breaking the code of conduct ?
A: Their character will most likely be deleted. We do this to protect the real players and make the un-serious players behave. We make these rules purely to help players. Its for the players own good.

Q: How will GM`s be able to spot people that breaks the code of conduct ?
A: Other players can report it and many of the things happening in EO will be logged. So GM`s will very easily catch the bad guys :)

Q: Does Code Of Conduct have anything to do with in game stuff like player killing ?
A: No absolutely not ! Just out of character things like harassment and creation of anti-philosophic groups, racism and so on.

Q: Will players have to role-play ?
A: We encourage role-playing of course. However, that's up to the player. But players may not stand in the middle of the street advertising for real life stuff or try selling pirate cds or stuff like that. This is not tolerated.

Q: How can players interact with the world ?
A: They can do very much with the world. They can pick up things, build things, make things, fish, chop down trees and much more.

Q: How does player interact with each other ?
A: They can speak, shout, whisper, emote and so on. They can fight with each other, trade with each other and much more.

Q: What transportation will there be ?
A: The traditional by foot method feet, water going ships, magic teleportation spells and more.

Q: Is combat turn based ?
A: No, its real time.

Q: How is the economy in the game ?
A: The economy will shift due to demand and supply in full version.
