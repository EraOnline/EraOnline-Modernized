The armour of the Elements

Legend tells of a man, who was a legendary blacksmith he made weapons for mortals and gods, and one time he forged the armour of the elements originally made to be given to the the god of griigo who was the god of wisdom and logic. But the armour was stolen and never seen again, rumour tells that the armour was taken to a cave located somewhere in the wastelands.
The thief apparently died many eternities ago, and all his treasures were left in this cave along with the Armour of the Elements.
The armour of the Elements was made magically, it was forged out of rock, but no ordinary rock, it was the Trillium rock, this was a very rare material and usual only found in small quantities. It was so special because it was light, Indestructible, and provides superb protection from all the elements Fire, Wind, Water, Earth and from the cold steel of swords.
But all is not so easy to get, first you must find this cave and then if legend tells truth, a dragon now lives their fierce and cold blooded, only brave and courageous sols will ever retrieve it.
The one that does finally wear this armour will be mighty, but as many wise men have said "One who wears no armour is just as mighty warrior as the one that does".
