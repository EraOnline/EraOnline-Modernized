Trough the entire history of Menath, the people within it has belived in
different gods and godesses. Some people dislike some deities and like others.
However, the official history of Menath support all the deities. This is to prevent
further arguments between the different religions. Here is a description of every
god and godess that the people in Menath belives in:

THE GODESS OF HYLIIOS

The godess of Hyliios stands for love and peace. She is loving, careing and
supportive to its followers. She belives in total freedom of mind and she has
no hate to any other factions.

THE GOD OF BENDARR

The god of war and evil. He belive that nothing good or fun comes from peace.
He want struggle, honor and blood. He stop at nothing to get what he want, but
the other gods usually manage to stop his evil acts. But when they don`t, the
world of Menath usually is hit hard.

THE GOD OF GRIIGO

Griigo is the god of logic and wisdom. He uses his powers to get wiser and
wiser, and to research the god of creations work. The other gods see Griigo
as a senile, old man, however, they often come to him for advices !

THE GODESS OF VEEGA

Veega is the godess of birth and death. She brings balance to the world of
Menath, and can be loved, and hated. She can take and she can give. She
can bring happiness and sorrow. The other deities respect her to the fullest.

THE GOD OF ZEENDIC

The god of work and commerce. He belive in total freedom of action. He 
belive people should do whatever they can to get money and respect. He is
very superficial, and the other deities looks upon him closely to watch his
moves. Cause he can betray them if he can earn anything on it.
