### Key Discoveries During Annotation
- **NPC movement types**: 1=stand, 2=random, 3=hostile chase (10 tiles), 4=guard chase criminals, 5=beggar follow, 6=tamed follow owner, 7=short-range hostile (3 tiles), 8=chaotic guard chase humans/wood elves
- **Guard types**: 1=normal (attack criminals only), 2=chaotic (attack humans+wood elves+criminals)
- **Hit calculation**: Swordmanship(Skill16) determines hit chance (>50 = always hit). Damage = random(MinHIT,MaxHIT) - DEF/2, minimum 1
- **Skill improvement**: 1/40 chance per combat swing for tactics/swordmanship/parrying to improve
- **RenderScreen**: 3-pass rendering (ground opaque, fringe+objects+weather transparent, characters composited as head+body+shield+weapon). Movement animation = 8px per frame offset reduction
- **Protocol messages identified**: CC (create char), CHC (change char), MP/MN (move player/NPC), BC (bye char), MOB/EOB (make/erase object), SIS/NIS/SPL (inventory/spell sync), ASS (stats), DEA (death), SUP (set position), PLW (play sound), and many more
