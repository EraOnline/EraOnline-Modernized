One of the most loved occupations in any roleplaying game is the Animal
Tamer. We would hate ourselves if we left this feature out in Era Online, so we
did not ! However, its still aboit shallow. Let me explain how it work.

WHAT YOU CAN DO WITH ANIMAL TAMING

* Tame ONE creature at a time
* Name the creature whatever you want (as long as its fantasy related)
* Have it follow you and instruct it

WHAT YOU WILL BE ABLE TO DO WITH ANIMAL TAMING SOON

* Have creatures fight for you
* Feed creatures
* Sell creatures
* Store stuff on creatures


HOW TO TAME A CREATURE

You are logged in as a fresh new animal tamer. "Lets tame a bear !", you think.
Sorry, thats NOT the way it goes. If you started the character as a animal tamer,
then you will have about 20 in animal taming skill. You cannot tame a bear with
this skill rate ! You will have to try taming smaller and less dangerous creatures.
The bigger and meaner a creature is, the more animal taming skill you need to
tame it. When you want to tame a creature, target it and type /TAME
The better skill you have, faster it will be tamed. Soon you will have a tamed
creature !

INSTRUCTING YOUR CREATURE

Creatures can be silly and you need to instruct them. Here is a list over all
commands you can do towards your creature:

/TAME                 = Tame the creature
/TRANSFER       = Transfer ownership of your creature to targeted player
/DISCARD          = You will loose ownership of your creature
/NAME 'name'    = Changes your creatures name to 'name'
/STOP                 = Your creature will stop following you until you tell it to follow.
/FOLLOW           = Your creature will start following you again
