  really a bug, but a lag problem. The code that draws the map
  to the client, isn't getting to the client, but by typinh /GFX
  the code is re-sent to the client.

  to do so, after clicking ENTER MENATH in the main menu. After

***5th FEBRUARY, 2000***

* LEVEL SYSTEM IS GONE. You still get experience from killing
  things, but you wont gain levels. The new system is simple 
  and should discourage power gaming. Once you get so and so 
  much experience, you get 5 training points and your stats go up.
  Stats: Hit power, health, stamina, mana etc. So you still got 
  to gain experience to get better.

* There is a caption on the main view that shows how much
  experience you have, and how much left before you get upgraded.
  That is raise stats, given training points etc.

* Game Master abilities have improven GREATLY. Game Masters can
  for example, now alter NPC`s hailing messages and names. They
  can also morph into other creatures etc. Game Masters can now
  create much more, and more entertaining quests !

* Monsters will no longer attack players which is too experienced
  for them. This is to prevent the common scenario of 5 spiderlings
  running after a powerful warrior. 

* Experience can now be gained in other ways than killing !
  You will gain experience from making things (tailoring,
  carpenting, cooking etc.) and using all other skills as
  well. We hope this will REALLY discouraging power gaming
  and players dont have to fight to get good anymore, so 
  that will make the game less hack n` slash.

* Some races can no longer be certain classes/occupations. This is
  to create greater balance between the races people play. For 
  example, Dark Elves cannot play clerics or animal tamers, and
  Haakis cannot play pirates or fishermen. Humans are the most
  flexible race to play. Also, CastleFall is the most popular
  startup city, and you should select this city to start in if
  you are of the human race. There is always people there to meet.

* You won't always get loot from creatures you killed. The more
  difficult the monster is, the less chance of finding loot.

* When you are being attacked or others are being attacked, you
  hear a screaming male or female voice depending on your
  character gender.

* SCHOOLS OF MAGIC ! The classes which can cast magic is now:
	-Druids		(NATURE)
	-Wizards	(DESTRUCTION)
	-Enchanters	(ENCHANTING)
	-Paladins	(ENCHANTING)
	-Clerics	(NATURE)
	-Healers	(NATURE)
 
 Spells are catagorized in these schools of magic. Your class
 must be a member of the school of magic the spell is a member
 of to cast it.

* Added some new spells:
	-The Ghosty Pain Wind
	-Necromantic Ressurection
	-Summon Tiny Pain
	-Hellfire

* Severe change to skill system. When creating a character, you
  must select three specialized skills. Only these can go over
  50 skill points ! Also, lets say your tailoring skill is the
  highest skill you have, your class will change to tailor, and
  if lumberjacking is the highest skill you have, your class 
  will change to woodworker. This goes for almost ALL the skills.
  You will just ahve to figure out yourself what skill will make
  you what. This system make players able to change their class
  in the game (they can pursue careers by working on skills).
  This is very good, since equipment is class oriented too now !

* The game will not tell you directly how much experience you
  gain when killing/performing a skill but you will see the
  caption on your main view change so you know how much experience
  you gained.

* BIG HOUSE OWNERSHIP UPDATE ! You can now lock your houses ! 
  To buy a house, locate an architect and buy a house deed of your
  choice. Read the deed by USING it. It will tell you to mail us
  so we can place the house for you and stuff like that. Now, when
  the house is placed, you will get one lock so you can lock your
  house. Stand in the door and type /LOCK to lock it. Now you are
  the only person that can move on this position. Type /UNLOCK to
  unlock the position again. This update was really needed. Now 
  players can furniture their homes, store goods there, keep animals
  there and so on, without having to worry about people going inside
  and stealing !

* SIGNS IMPLENTED. You can now buy signs at any wood worker. When you
  drop a sign, you cannot pick it up again, so be sure where you want
  the sign to be, before dropping it. Once dropped, click on it to
  change its text. The sign will stay there forever, and everyone who
  click on the sign will see what you typed. This is great for guild
  signs, and signs outside your home etc.

* Big animal taming update ! Animals you have tamed will now reappear
  with you when you log in again. You will be greeted by this message:
  "Your tamed animal looks happy to see you !". In closed beta, you
  would loose your animal when logging off, this is no longer the 
  case ! We will also soon make a feature, so your animals can attack
  the targets you want it to attack. For now, animals cannot help you
  fight.

* Hiding skill fixed. The skill will make you TOTALLY invisible. There
  was a problem in closed beta, which made the user`s body visible but
  not the head.

* You can now hail NPC`s. By targeting any NPC and typing /HAIL the
  npc will greet you back. If the NPC turns out to be just another
  common NPC, the NPC will greet you with nothing special. However,
  if the NPC turns out to have a quest or any other form special
  tips or information, the NPC will tell you so. Always use the
  /HAIL command when encountering a new NPC. You won`t belive what
  you may find. Gamemasters are able to change the hailing messages
  for NPC`s, so if you have greeted one NPC before, greet the NPC 
  again, cause the NPC may suddenly have something you really want !

* There is a timer on your criminal flag now. Whenever you break
  the law (pickpocket and get cought, attacking players etc.) 
  some minutes will be added to the timer. Like attacking a player
  will add 45 minutes to the timer, and it will take 45 minutes
  before you are being unflagged as a criminal. If you commit
  another crime before the time runs out, the new time will be
  added to the current time. If you log out, and log back in,
  the timer will be reset and you will have to "serve" the time
  from the start again. We need to punish those who brake laws!
  
* How much experience you get from using skills and making
  stuff is still to be tested. We can tweak it until everyone
  is satisfied with it. You will earn more experience by doing
  some things, and less doing some other things.

* No more tombstones when dying ! The new system works like this:
  When a player die, a random item from the players backpack is
  selected and dropped to the ground where the player died instead
  of a tombstone. So now the player will have to find the item 
  again before anyone else finds it.

* No more level restrictions on equipment. BUT there is Health (strength)
  restrictions. Some weapons, shields and armor need a certain amount of
  Health to wear ! However, all clothing is free for everyone to use.

* When praying to priests, the gods and goddesses still grant a steady
  amount of stuff. However, after you reach a certain rate in experience,
  gold and health they will not grant you anymore.

* Major Merchant Skill/Trading update ! If you have over 70 in merchant
  skill (remember, only three specialized skills can go over 50 !), you
  will start EARNING money when selling stuff to NPC`s. That means, you
  will get more money that the item is worth. Also, you will get items
  cheaper than what its worth when buying items. This encourage players
  to be merchants. Buy low and sell high !

* Two new special objects added ! Small Merchant Booth and Grand Merchant
  Booth. These can be seen in Castlefall centre, and now you can buy one
  yourself. There is nothing special with these items, but they look cool
  if you are a merchant. You can drop it, stand behind it and act like a 
  real merchant ! These two new objects can be found at a woodworker.

* Im working on getting the economy system much better. I want to create
  a demand and supply system as soon as possible so there is a points in
  travelling around selling stuff for merchant players.

* All equipment are now class oriented ! That means that some equipment
  can not be used by some classes. The system is pretty self explanatory.
  Clerics cannot use heavy armor, warriors can not wear paladin suits,
  paladins cannot wear drity clothing, mages cannot use heavy swords,
  thieves cannot use heavy shields etc.

* Titles and names has been abit re-arranged. When clicking on another
  player the name, title and class will appear like this for example:
  "Jack The Fameous Woodworker".

* Snow is now implented. There is still a little silly thing, it actually
  rain and snow in the desert, but lets just say that Menath is a very
  unstable world ! It will rain in the summer, and snow in the winther.

* Attacking guards will make you a criminal instantly.

* BAN SYSTEM. We needed it badly really. Players can now be banned by
  GameMasters. When you are banned, you cannot enter the game with 
  an old character or a new character when banned. GameMasters will 
  only ban players directly breaking the code of conduct. If you wish
  to complain on a ban, mail support@eraonline.net

* New credits screen implented. Now much more better looking.
  Take a look, maybe you are under SPECIAL THANKS TO :)

* Dropping 0 gold coins no longer works.

* Grape Juice, Cyclop Blood Ale and Snake Wine now works.

* Creatures will now give MUCH MUCH more experience. For instance,
  a grown up cyclop will give you 1200 experience points !

* You will not know how much experience you gain for killing
  and doing stuff. You will just have to hope that you get
  much experience from killing so and so. Ask around to find 
  out what you should kill. Sooner or later, your stats will
  go up.

* Defence bugs are fixed. Equipping shields, armor etc. and
  unequipping them now works totally fine.

* Dead players logging back on will have ghost head as well
  as ghost body. There was a bug that made players have their
  original heads and ghost body when logging in.

* Dead players will not loose health or stamina in rain.

* Players under level 5 cannot cast destructive spells on
  players over level 5 and vice versa.

* Hit power of warrior sword has been turned severely down.

* Rusty Paladin Sword, Paladin Sword and Fancy Paladin Sword
  has now proper hit points.

* Meditation speed should be improved.

* Weapons, shields and clothing displays properly in
  the character sheet. Even when logging back in.

* The /WHO command is back due to demand. Type /WHO to see
  names of all players online.

* When clicking on a criminal player, their name will appear
  in red.

* Skills will not naturally raise themselves until they are 
  10 or over. This is to make players work more and invest
  skill points in what they want to really work with.

* Guards are now called Guards instead of Militas.

* Guards will no longer kill non-criminals. This was a big
  bug, but it is totally fixed now.

* Dark Elf guards are chaotic. They will attack Humans and Wood
  Elves. Dark Elves guards will also kill criminals of all 
  races just as the human, wood elf and haaki guards.

* Swordmanship and Tactics are now very valuable. The better 
  swordmanship skill you have, better is the chance you will
  hit your foe (no matter what kind of weapon you use). And
  the better Tactics skill you have, the lesser chance your
  foe has to hit you.

* Bandages implented. They can be bought from healers. By
  targeting someone, yourself you want to, and then USING
  the bandage, you will heal the target abit. The better
  healing skill you have, the faster laying the bandages goes.

* Evaluating weapons, shields and armor will also tell you 
  how much hit/defence the item gives you.

* You can now roast fish just as meat.
