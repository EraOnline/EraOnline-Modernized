Decollo Patria

Great legends are born every day, this is no exception, a story of adventure, of courage, of noble knights who fought for the right to live but died in honour, and so my story begins.

Legends tell of an island that lies to the north east of the great city of DarkRoam, on this island once a great civilisation existed and thrived for many eternity and reached its evolutionary peak.
But one day a knight arrived known as the Red Knight who spread terror throughout the island and killed many innocent souls, then 4 brave warriors strode forth to confront the evil knight. A great battle was fought here known as the battle of souls, but although these knights were brave and hardy they were killed as the rest of this civilisation was, by beheading.
This red knight made his home here and was later found out to be the servant of the God of Destruction who left him here to continue his rain of terror.
The knight has never left the island named Decollo Patria meaning Beheaded Homeland, others have challenged him but were not of strong and of pure heart to kill him and were added to the pile of rotting heads the Red Knight keeps.
Only one brave soul of pure heart can challenge this mighty knight, and restore honour to those who have died by the knight’s hand. This courageous Hero if he succeeds will be rewarded with great riches.
And most of all stop this Knight of pure cold evil that has kept terror on this land.
